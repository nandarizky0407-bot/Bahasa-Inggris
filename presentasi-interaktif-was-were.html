Tujuan

Apa yang Akan Kita Pelajari Hari Ini? 🎯

Ada 5 hal seru yang menantimu. Siap? Ayo mulai!

🗣️

1. Melafalkan

Ucapkan 30 kata perasaan yang sering dipakai setiap hari: happy, sad, angry, scared, excited, proud, dan masih banyak lagi. Klik kartu untuk mendengarkannya!

🔵

2. Kata Lampau “Was”

Was adalah bentuk lampau dari is/am, untuk I, He, She, It: “I was happy yesterday.” — Aku bahagia kemarin.

🟠

3. Kata Lampau “Were”

Were adalah bentuk lampau dari are, untuk You, We, They: “We were tired last night.” — Kami lelah semalam.

❓

4. Bertanya & Menjawab

Tanya perasaan dengan Was he...? atau Were you...? lalu jawab Yes, I was ✅ atau No, I wasn't ❌.

🏆

5. Berlatih & Bermain

Baca percakapan, isi latihan berbicara, kerjakan kuis 10 soal, dan baca fabel seru tentang Ollie si gurita yang kesepian!

💡

Tips Penting

Kata penanda waktu lalu: yesterday, last night, last week. Kalau waktunya sudah lewat, pakai was atau were, ya!

Materi 1

Was: Cerita Perasaan Kemarin 🔵

Was adalah bentuk lampau dari is dan am. Pakai was untuk menceritakan perasaan yang sudah lewat: kemarin, tadi pagi, minggu lalu!

📌 Rumus: I / He / She / It + was + kata sifat perasaan
kata sifat perasaan: happy, sad, angry, tired, scared, ...
Siapa? 👤Contoh Kalimat 💬
I
“I was so happy at the party.” 🎉
I → was (bukan is atau am!)
He, She,
It
“Dita was tired last night.” 😫
Dita = she → was
💡 Jurus hafal cepat: was untuk satu orang, seekor, atau satu benda — I, he, she, it. I was happy. ✅ — bukan I were happy ❌! Kata sifat perasaan langsung mengikuti was: was + happy / sad / tired.
Materi 2

Were: Kamu, Kami, dan Mereka 🟠

Were adalah bentuk lampau dari are. Pakai were untuk You, We, They — atau bila orangnya lebih dari satu!

📌 Rumus: You / We / They + were + kata sifat perasaan
you tunggal maupun jamak → tetap were

😫 Lelah Setelah Bermain

Kami bermain sepak bola tadi siang, jadi badan kami lelah sekali.

“We were tired after the game.”

🐒 Senang di Kebun Binatang

Mereka melihat banyak hewan lucu di kebun binatang kemarin.

“They were happy at the zoo.”

🥱 Mengantuk Pagi Hari

Kamu tidur terlalu malam, jadi pagi tadi kamu mengantuk.

“You were sleepy this morning.”
⚠️ Hati-hati! You selalu berpasangan dengan were, walaupun hanya satu orang: You were late. ✅. Dan jangan pernah menulis we was ❌ atau they was ❌ — We, You, They → were!
Materi 3

Bertanya Perasaan: Was I...? Were You...? ❓

Mau tahu perasaan temanmu kemarin? Letakkan was atau were di DEPAN kalimat!

📌 Rumus Tanya: Was / Were + Subject + kata sifat ?
wasn't = was not • weren't = were not
Pertanyaan ❓Jawaban: Ya ✅Jawaban: Tidak ❌
“Were you scared last night?” 😨
“Yes, I was.” ✅
“No, I wasn't.” ❌
“Was she happy at the party?” 🎉
“Yes, she was.” ✅
“No, she wasn't.” ❌
🌈 Tanya perasaan dengan kata tanya: “How was your day?” (Bagaimana harimu?) atau “How were you yesterday?” → “It was great!” 😄

Were dalam Kalimat 📖

Semua kalimat ini untuk You, We, atau They. Coba perhatikan: were berpasangan dengan banyak orang!

Mereka senang di kebun binatang.

My friends were sad on the last day of school.
Teman-temanku sedih di hari terakhir sekolah.
We were hungry after the long trip.
Kami lapar setelah perjalanan yang panjang.
They were bored because the movie was long.
Mereka bosan karena filmnya panjang.

Ollie the Lonely Octopus 🐙

Sebuah fabel tentang gurita kecil yang kesepian karena ia berbeda. Kata berwarna kuning bisa diklik! Klik ▶ untuk mendengarkan ceritanya.

Ollie is a little octopus with eight long arms. Yesterday, Ollie was very sad. He was lonely because the small fish were scared of his arms. “Why am I different?” he cried. His heart was heavy, and his eyes were wet. He was worried that he would never have a friend. 🐙

Then, one sunny morning, a loud cry came. Toto the little fish was stuck between two rocks! He was very scared, and his voice was shaking. “Help! Help!” The other fish were too scared to come near. But Ollie was brave! His eight arms were strong and quick. He moved the rocks, and Toto was free. The fish were so surprised — and so happy! 🎉

Now, Ollie is never lonely. After that day, the sea friends were kind to him. Every afternoon, the little fish were excited to play with him. “Your arms were very brave yesterday!” they say. Ollie was proud, and his heart was light. He was not different in a bad way — he was different in a wonderful way! 🐙✨

🌱 Moral of the story: Sad days do not last. Be brave and kind — happy days will come!Hari sedih tidak bertahan lama. Beranilah menolong — hari bahagia pasti datang!

Kesimpulan

Ingat 3 Hal Ini! 📌

Ini rangkuman materi hari ini. Simpan baik-baik di kepalamu, ya!

🔵 Was (I/He/She/It)

  • was = bentuk lampau dari is/am
  • Untuk: I, he, she, it
  • I was happy yesterday.
  • Satu orang / satu benda → was!

🟠 Were (You/We/They)

  • were = bentuk lampau dari are
  • Untuk: you, we, they
  • We were tired last night.
  • You tunggal maupun jamak → were!

😊 30 Kata Perasaan

  • Senang: happy, glad, excited, proud, brave
  • Sedih: sad, angry, scared, lonely, shy
  • Tubuh: hungry, thirsty, sick, tired
  • Dan masih banyak lagi!
🎯 I / he / she / it → was  •  You / we / they → were  •  Bertanya → Were you...? / How was your day?  •  Dan ingat: perasaan bisa berubah! 😊✨

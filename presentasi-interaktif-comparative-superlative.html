2. Lebih (-ER)

Gunakan taller, bigger, faster + than untuk membandingkan DUA benda atau orang.

👑

3. Paling (THE + -EST)

Gunakan the tallest, the biggest, the happiest untuk membandingkan BANYAK benda (3 atau lebih).

🚂

4. Kata Panjang

Kata sifat panjang naik kereta: pakai more (lebih) dan the most (paling) — more beautiful, the most expensive!

✨

5. Kata Ajaib

Hafal bentuk tak beraturan: good → better → best, bad → worse → worst, more, most, less, least!

Materi 1

Lebih... : Tambahkan -ER 📈

Comparative dipakai untuk membandingkan DUA benda atau orang. Ada 4 aturan ejaannya!

1️⃣ Kata pendek → + ER

tall → taller 📏
fast → faster 🏃
long → longer

“A train is faster than a bus.” 🚄

2️⃣ Berakhiran -E → cukup + R

nice → nicer 😊
large → larger
safe → safer

“This bag is nicer than that one.” 👜

3️⃣ Konsonan–Vokal–Konsonan → GANDAKAN + ER

big → bigger 🐘
hot → hotter 🔥
thin → thinner

“An elephant is bigger than a horse.” 🐴

4️⃣ Berakhiran -Y → GANTI jadi IER

happy → happier 😄
easy → easier
funny → funnier 😂

“Cio is happier today.” 🎉
📈 Rumus Lebih: A + is/are + kata sifat-ER + THAN + B “Rian is taller than Dita.”
💡 THAN artinya “daripada”. Jangan lupa pakai than setiap kali kamu membandingkan dua benda atau dua orang, ya!
Materi 2

Paling... : Tambahkan THE + -EST 👑

Superlative dipakai untuk membandingkan BANYAK benda (3 atau lebih). Aturan ejanya sama dengan -ER!

1️⃣ Kata pendek → THE + EST

the tallest 👑
the fastest
the longest

“The cheetah is the fastest animal.” 🐆

2️⃣ Berakhiran -E → + ST

the nicest
the largest
the safest

“This is the nicest bag in the shop.” 👜

3️⃣ Konsonan–Vokal–Konsonan → GANDAKAN + EST

the biggest
the hottest
the thinnest

“July is the hottest month.” ☀️

4️⃣ Berakhiran -Y → IEST

the happiest
the easiest
the funniest

“Today is the happiest day!” 🎉
👑 Rumus Paling: A + is/are + THE + kata sifat-EST + in/of ... “Rian is the tallest in his class.”
⚠️ Superlative SELALU memakai THE 👑 — the tallest, the biggest, the happiest. Tanpa THE, kalimatmu jadi tergelincir! 😄
Materi 3

Kata Panjang: MORE & THE MOST 🚂

Kata sifat yang panjang (2 suku kata atau lebih) tidak pakai -er/-est. Dia naik kereta MORE dan THE MOST!

MORE = lebih 🌹

more beautiful = lebih cantik
more expensive = lebih mahal
more difficult = lebih sulit

“Gold is more expensive than silver.”

THE MOST = paling 👸

the most beautiful = paling cantik
the most expensive = paling mahal
the most difficult = paling sulit

“Diamonds are the most expensive.” 💎
🚂 Rumus Kata Panjang: MORE + kata sifat (lebih) • THE MOST + kata sifat (paling) “This movie is more exciting than that one.”
💡 Contoh kata panjang: beautiful, expensive, difficult, exciting, interesting, delicious. Jangan pernah bilang “beautifuller”, ya! 😄
Materi 4

Kata-kata ini TIDAK ikut aturan -er/-est. Bentuknya berubah total — harus dihafal! Klik 🔊 untuk mendengar.

Pip the Smallest Mouse 🐭

Sebuah fabel tentang tiga sahabat di Green Forest. Kata berwarna kuning bisa diklik! Klik ▶ untuk mendengarkan ceritanya.

Rex the rabbit, Ellie the elephant, and Pip the mouse are best friends in Green Forest. Rex is the fastest runner. Ellie is the biggest and the strongest animal. And Pip? He is the smallest and the quietest of them all. One sunny day, the three friends find a jar of honey between two big rocks. “Yum! This is the sweetest honey in the forest!” says Rex.

The jar is inside a narrow crack. Rex tries first. He is fast, but the crack is too narrow for his big paws. “Ouch!” Then Ellie pushes the rocks with her trunk. She is the strongest, but the rocks do not move. “This is harder than I thought!” she sighs. The three friends sit down sadly. The honey is so close, but nobody can reach it.

Then Pip quietly stands up. “Let me try. I am the smallest, so I fit in narrow places!” He walks into the crack and pushes the jar out slowly. “Hooray for Pip!” Rex and Ellie shout happily. “You are small, but today you are the best of us all!” Then the three friends share the sweetest honey together. 🍯

🌱 Moral of the story: Everyone has their own strength — even the smallest one!Setiap orang punya kelebihan masing-masing, sekecil apa pun ukurannya.

Kesimpulan

Ingat 3 Kelompok Kata Ini! 📌

Ini rangkuman materi hari ini. Simpan baik-baik di kepalamu, ya!

📈 Lebih (-ER) + THAN

  • kata pendek: tall → taller
  • berakhir -e: nice → nicer
  • big → bigger (gandakan)
  • happy → happier (y → ier)
  • selalu pakai than

👑 Paling (THE + -EST)

  • the tallest, the nicest
  • the biggest, the happiest
  • selalu pakai the
  • untuk 3 benda atau lebih
  • in / of di akhir kalimat

🌈 Panjang & Kata Ajaib

  • panjang: more / the most
  • good → better → best
  • bad → worse → worst
  • many → more → most
  • little → less → least
  • far → farther → farthest
🎯 Pendek → -er / -est  •  Panjang → more / the most  •  Ajaib → better/best, worse/worst, more/most, less/least

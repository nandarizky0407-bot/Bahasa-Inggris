Materi 1

Should: Saran yang Baik ✅

Should artinya “sebaiknya” atau “seharusnya”. Kita memakainya untuk memberi nasihat dan saran yang baik!

📌 Rumus: Subject + should + Verb 1
kata kerjanya TIDAK pernah berubah!
Siapa? 👤Contoh Kalimat 💬
I, You,
We, They
“We should wash our hands before eating.” 🧼
should + wash (bentuk dasar)
He, She, It
“Rina should brush her teeth twice a day.” 🪥
tetap brush, BUKAN brushes!
💡 Jurus hafal cepat: setelah should, kata kerja selalu BENTUK DASAR. should wash ✅ — bukan should washes ❌, bukan should to wash ❌, dan bukan should washing ❌!
Materi 2

Shouldn't: Jangan Lakukan Itu! ❌

Shouldn't = should not. Artinya “jangan” atau “sebaiknya jangan” — untuk melarang hal yang kurang baik.

📌 Rumus: Subject + shouldn't + Verb 1
shouldn't = should not

🗑️ Jangan Buang Sampah

Sampah harus masuk ke tempatnya, bukan di lantai atau di sungai.

“You shouldn't throw trash on the floor.”

🚰 Hemat Air

Jangan biarkan keran mengalir terus — air itu berharga!

“He shouldn't leave the faucet open.”

🚽 Jangan Lupa Siram

Setelah pakai toilet, selalu siram supaya tetap bersih.

“She shouldn't forget to flush.”
⚠️ Hati-hati! Shouldn't adalah bentuk singkat dari should not. Keduanya sama saja: You shouldn't litter = You should not litter. Dan ingat: setelah shouldn't, kata kerja juga tetap bentuk dasar — shouldn't throw ✅, bukan shouldn't throws ❌!
Materi 3

Bertanya Saran: Should I...? ❓

Saat kita tidak tahu apa yang baik untuk dilakukan, kita bertanya dengan should. Letakkan should di DEPAN kalimat!

📌 Rumus Tanya: Should + Subject + Verb 1 ?
Pertanyaan ❓Jawaban: Ya ✅Jawaban: Tidak ❌
“Should I wash my hands now?” 🧼
“Yes, you should.” ✅
“No, you shouldn't.” ❌
“Should she cut her nails?” ✂️
“Yes, she should.” ✅
“No, she shouldn't.” ❌
🆘 Kalau bingung mau apa, tanyakan: “What should I do?” (Apa yang sebaiknya aku lakukan?) → “You should clean your desk.” 🧹
Contoh Kalimat

Should dalam Kalimat 📖

Klik tombol 🔊 untuk mendengar setiap kalimat, lalu ulangi dengan keras! Semuanya saran yang baik, ya!

You should wash your hands with soap before eating.
Kamu sebaiknya mencuci tangan dengan sabun sebelum makan.
We should brush our teeth twice a day.
Kita sebaiknya menyikat gigi dua kali sehari.
She should take a bath every morning.
Dia sebaiknya mandi setiap pagi.
You should throw your trash into the trash bin.
Kamu sebaiknya membuang sampahmu ke tempat sampah.
We should keep our classroom clean and tidy.
Kita sebaiknya menjaga kelas tetap bersih dan rapi.
He should drink clean water every day.
Dia sebaiknya minum air bersih setiap hari.
Contoh Kalimat

Shouldn't dalam Kalimat 📖

Semua kalimat ini larangan. Coba perhatikan: kata kerjanya tetap bentuk dasar!

You shouldn't throw rubbish in the river.
Kamu jangan membuang sampah di sungai.
He shouldn't leave the faucet open.
Dia jangan membiarkan keran terbuka.
You shouldn't walk on the wet floor.
Kamu jangan berjalan di lantai yang basah.
She shouldn't forget to flush the toilet.
Dia jangan lupa menyiram toilet.
We shouldn't share a toothbrush with our friends.
Kita jangan berbagi sikat gigi dengan teman.
They shouldn't spit on the floor.
Mereka jangan meludah di lantai.
Contoh Kalimat

Bertanya Saran dalam Kalimat 📖

Ungkapan ini sangat berguna saat kamu ragu harus berbuat apa. Coba ucapkan!

Should I wash my hands now?
Apakah aku sebaiknya mencuci tangan sekarang?
What should I do with this dirty tissue?
Apa yang sebaiknya aku lakukan dengan tisu kotor ini?
Yes, you should brush your teeth before bed.
Ya, kamu sebaiknya menggosok gigi sebelum tidur.
No, you shouldn't drink that dirty water!
Tidak, kamu jangan minum air kotor itu!
Should we bring a towel to school?
Apakah kita perlu membawa handuk ke sekolah?
You should be proud of your clean room!
Kamu patut bangga dengan kamarmu yang bersih!

Story Time

Momo the Messy Monkey 🐒

Sebuah fabel tentang monyet kecil yang malas membersihkan diri. Kata berwarna kuning bisa diklik! Klik ▶ untuk mendengarkan ceritanya.

Momo is a little monkey. He lives in a tall mango tree in the green jungle. Momo is cute, but he is very messy. He never washes his hands, he never brushes his teeth, and his room is full of banana skins and trash. His mother always says, “Momo, you should take a bath every day. You should clean your room!” But Momo always answers, “Later, Mom! I want to play!” 🍌

One sunny day, Momo's friends come to play. “Eww! Your fur is dirty and your breath smells bad, Momo!” says Lala the rabbit. “We shouldn't play with a dirty monkey,” says Toto the tiger, and they all go away. Momo feels very sad. That night, his tooth hurts too. “Ouch! Ouch!” The doctor says, “You shouldn't eat too many sweets. You should brush your teeth twice a day, and you should keep your room tidy.” 🦷

Momo listens to the doctor. He takes a bath, brushes his teeth, and sweeps his room. He wipes the floor and throws all the trash into the trash bin. The next morning, his friends come back. “Wow! Momo, you smell so nice, and your room is so clean!” they say. Now Momo is happy and healthy. He should be very proud — and he is! 🐒✨

🌱 Moral of the story: Clean body, clean room, happy friends!Tubuh bersih, kamar bersih, teman-teman pun bahagia!

Kesimpulan

Ingat 3 Hal Ini! 📌

Ini rangkuman materi hari ini. Simpan baik-baik di kepalamu, ya!

✅ Should (+)

  • Artinya: sebaiknya / seharusnya
  • Rumus: Subject + should + Verb 1
  • You should wash your hands.
  • Kata kerja tetap bentuk dasar!

❌ Shouldn't (−)

  • shouldn't = should not
  • Artinya: jangan
  • You shouldn't litter.
  • Kata kerjanya juga bentuk dasar!

🧼 30 Kata Kebersihan

  • Diri: wash, soap, bath, brush, towel
  • Rumah: sweep, mop, wipe, tidy, trash bin
  • Sehat: germs, faucet, flush, healthy
  • Dan masih banyak lagi!
🎯 Saran baik → should + Verb 1  •  Larangan → shouldn't + Verb 1  •  Bertanya → Should I...? / What should I do?  •  Dan ingat: keep it clean! 🧼✨

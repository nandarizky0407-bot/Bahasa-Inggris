Lesson 2 · Have vs Has

The Have / Has Rule ⚖️

Both show possession — but which one? It depends on the subject!

HAVE ✅

Use with: I, You, We, They and plural subjects.

• Isaya have two eyesmata.

• Youkamu have a bluebiru bagtas.

• Wekami have threetiga catskucing.

• Theymereka have five booksbuku.

HAS ✅

Use with: He, She, It and singular subjects.

• Shedia (pr) has longpanjang hairrambut.

• Hedia (lk) has a redmerah bicyclesepeda.

• The elephantgajah has bigbesar earstelinga.

• Ititu has four legskaki.

🧠 Quick Practice — Choose have or has!

1. Andi has a black pencil. (He → has)
2. Sinta and I have new books. (We → have)
3. The tiger has sharp teeth. (It → has)
4. My friends have three cats. (They → have)

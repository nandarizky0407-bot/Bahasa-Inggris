Grammar Lesson

Simple Present Tense 📚

Learn how to make positive, negative, and question sentences about daily activities!

✅ Positive (+)

I/You/We/They + V1
He/She/It + V1+s/es

I walk to school.
(Saya berjalan ke sekolah.)
She reads a book.
(Dia membaca buku.)
They play football.
(Mereka bermain sepak bola.)

❌ Negative (-)

I/You/We/They + don't + V1
He/She/It + doesn't + V1

I don't walk to school.
(Saya tidak berjalan ke sekolah.)
She doesn't read a book.
(Dia tidak membaca buku.)
They don't play football.
(Mereka tidak bermain sepak bola.)

❓ Question (?)

Do + I/you/we/they + V1?
Does + he/she/it + V1?

Do you walk to school?
(Apakah kamu berjalan ke sekolah?)
Does she read a book?
(Apakah dia membaca buku?)
Do they play football?
(Apakah mereka bermain sepak bola?)

🧠 Quick Summary

Positive:

I/You/We/They → V1

He/She/It → V1+s/es

Negative:

I/You/We/They → don't + V1

He/She/It → doesn't + V1

Question:

Do + I/you/we/they + V1?

Does + he/she/it + V1?

Grammar Lesson 1

Simple Future Tense 🚀

We use Simple Future Tense for actions that will happen in the future.

✅ Positive (+)

Subject + will + V1

I will play football.
(Saya akan bermain sepak bola.)
She will go to school.
(Dia akan pergi ke sekolah.)

❌ Negative (-)

Subject + will not (won't) + V1

I won't play football.
(Saya tidak akan bermain sepak bola.)
She won't go to school.
(Dia tidak akan pergi ke sekolah.)

❓ Question (?)

Will + Subject + V1?

Will you play football?
(Apakah kamu akan bermain sepak bola?)
Will she go to school?
(Apakah dia akan pergi ke sekolah?)

🧠 Time Signals (Keterangan Waktu)

tomorrow next week next month next year later soon tonight

Important: After "will" or "won't", we always use the Base Verb (V1)!
Example: "She will go" (NOT "will goes" or "will went").

Grammar Lesson 2

Short Answers & "Will Be" 💬

Learn how to answer quickly and use "will be" for future facts!

Short Answers

We use will or won't in short answers.

Will you come? → Yes, I will. / No, I won't.
(Apakah kamu akan datang? Ya/Tidak.)
Will she cook? → Yes, she will. / No, she won't.
(Apakah dia akan memasak? Ya/Tidak.)

Using "Will Be"

Use will be for jobs, situations, or conditions in the future.

I will be a doctor.
(Saya akan menjadi seorang dokter.)
It will be rainy tomorrow.
(Besok akan menjadi hari yang hujan.)

🧠 Let's Compare!

Will + Action (V1):
He will play football tomorrow.
Will be + Noun/Adjective:
He will be a great player.

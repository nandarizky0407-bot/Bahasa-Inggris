The Ant and the Dove

One hot morning, a little ant was very thirsty. She walked to the river to drink. The river was near her home in the forest.

Oh no! The water pulled her, and the little ant fell into the river. A dove sat on a tree near the river. He saw the ant. Quickly, he picked a big leaf and dropped it into the water. The ant climbed onto the leaf. She was safe!

A few days later, a hunter came to the forest. He pointed his net at the dove. The ant saw it. She bit the hunter's foot. "Ouch!" cried the hunter, and he ran away. The dove was safe too.

🧭 Pertanyaan Menuntun

Moral: One good turn deserves another.

Contoh kesimpulan: The story is about a little ant and a dove. The dove helped the ant by dropping a leaf into the river so the ant could climb on it. Later, the ant helped the dove by biting the hunter's foot. We learn that kindness comes back to us — when we help others, they will help us too.

The Rabbit and the Turtle

A rabbit had strong legs. He could run very fast. A turtle walked very, very slowly. The rabbit always laughed at the turtle. "You are so slow!" he said.

One day, the turtle said, "Let us have a race!" The rabbit laughed, but he agreed. "Ready... go!" The rabbit ran very fast. The turtle walked slowly, step by step.

The rabbit was far ahead, so he stopped and slept under a tree. The turtle did not stop. He walked and walked. When the rabbit woke up, he ran as fast as he could — but it was too late. The turtle was already at the finish line!

🧭 Pertanyaan Menuntun

Moral: Slow but steady wins the race.

Contoh kesimpulan: The story is about a race between a fast rabbit and a slow turtle. The rabbit was lazy and over-confident, so he slept in the middle of the race. The turtle kept walking and never gave up, so he won. We learn that hard work and consistency are more important than speed — never be lazy or look down on others.

The Thirsty Crow

One hot day, a crow was very thirsty. He flew here and there, but he could not find any water. He almost gave up.

Then he saw a jar under a tree! There was a little water inside. He put his beak into the jar, but the water was too low. He could not reach it.

The clever crow looked around. He saw many small stones. He picked them up, one by one, and dropped them into the jar. Slowly, the water came up higher and higher. At last, the crow drank the water happily and flew home.

🧭 Pertanyaan Menuntun

Moral: Where there is a will, there is a way.

Contoh kesimpulan: The story is about a thirsty crow. The water in the jar was too low, but the clever crow did not give up. He dropped small stones into the jar until the water came up, and then he drank it. We learn that when we face a problem, we should think cleverly and keep trying — there is always a way.

The Fox and the Grapes

A fox was walking in the forest. He was very hungry. Then he saw a tree full of sweet grapes. The grapes were high, high up on the tree.

"Mmm, those grapes look delicious!" said the fox. He jumped and jumped, but the grapes were too high. He jumped ten times, but he still could not reach them.

The fox was tired and angry. He walked away and said, "Huh! Those grapes are sour. I don't want them!" But deep in his heart, he really wanted those sweet grapes.

🧭 Pertanyaan Menuntun

Moral: It is easy to hate what you cannot get.

Contoh kesimpulan: The story is about a hungry fox who wanted some grapes. The grapes were too high, so he could not reach them. When he gave up, he lied and said the grapes were sour. We learn that we should not say bad things about something just because we cannot get it — do not lie to yourself.

📖 Reading Practice • Cerita 5 dari 5
👦🐺

The Boy Who Cried Wolf

A shepherd boy sat near the village and watched his sheep. Every day was the same. He felt bored.

"I know!" he said. He ran to the village and shouted, "Wolf! Wolf! A wolf is chasing my sheep!" All the people ran to help him — but there was no wolf. The boy laughed loudly. He did it again the next day, and the people were very angry.

One day, a real wolf came! The boy shouted, "Wolf! Wolf! Please help me!" But nobody came. "He is lying again," they said. The wolf chased away many sheep. The boy cried, but it was too late.

🧭 Pertanyaan Menuntun

Moral: Liars will not be believed, even when they tell the truth.

Contoh kesimpulan: The story is about a shepherd boy who lied two times. He shouted "Wolf!" when there was no wolf, just to have fun. When a real wolf came, nobody believed him anymore, so he lost his sheep. We learn that we must always be honest — people will not trust a liar, even when he tells the truth.

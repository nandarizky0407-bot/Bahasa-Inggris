Grammar Lesson 1

What is an Adjective? 🧐

An adjective is a word that describes a noun or pronoun. It tells us what kind, how many, or which one.

1. Before a Noun

Adjective + Noun

It is a beautiful flower.
(Ini adalah bunga yang indah.)
He is a smart boy.
(Dia anak laki-laki yang pintar.)

2. After "To Be"

Subject + am/is/are/was/were/will be + Adjective

The flower is beautiful.
(Bunga itu indah.)
The boy is smart.
(Anak laki-laki itu pintar.)

🧠 Common Expressions with Adjectives

Be careful! (Hati-hati!)
Don't be sad. (Jangan sedih.)
I am happy. (Saya senang.)
Grammar Lesson 2

Adjectives in 5 Tenses ⏰

Adjectives can be used in any tense. Let's see how!

1. Simple Present

Fact or habit

The big dog barks every night.
(Anjing besar itu menggonggong setiap malam.)

2. Present Continuous

Happening now

The smart boy is reading a book.
(Anak pintar itu sedang membaca buku.)

3. Simple Past

Finished in the past

The old house was dirty yesterday.
(Rumah tua itu kotor kemarin.)

4. Present Perfect

Connects past to now

She has been happy today.
(Dia sudah bahagia hari ini.)

5. Simple Future

Will happen

Tomorrow will be a hot day.
(Besok akan menjadi hari yang panas.)
Grammar Lesson 3

Asking About Feelings 💭

We can use adjectives to ask about someone's condition or feelings. Let's learn the formula!

The Formula: To Be + Subject + Adjective + ?

To ask a question, put the "To Be" verb at the beginning of the sentence!

Are you happy?
(Apakah kamu bahagia?)
Is he tired?
(Apakah dia lelah?)

Short Answers

Are you happy? → Yes, I am. / No, I am not.
Is she sick? → Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

Using "Why" and "How"

Why are you angry?
(Kenapa kamu marah?)
How are you today?
(Apa kabarmu hari ini?)

⏰ Asking in 5 Tenses

1️⃣ Simple Present: Are you happy? (Apakah kamu bahagia?)
2️⃣ Present Continuous: Are you feeling happy? (Apakah kamu sedang merasa bahagia?)
3️⃣ Simple Past: Were you sad yesterday? (Apakah kamu sedih kemarin?)
4️⃣ Present Perfect: Have you been busy? (Apakah kamu sudah sibuk?)
5️⃣ Simple Future: Will you be angry? (Apakah kamu akan marah?)

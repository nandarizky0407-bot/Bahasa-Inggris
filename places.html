🗣️ Percakapan Panjang (Review Gabungan)

Tias: "Good morning, Leo! How are you doing today?"
Leo: "Good morning, Tias! I am fine. I am eight years old now. Happy to see you!"
Tias: "Happy to see you too! Look, I have one new blue bicycle. I go to school using this bicycle every morning."
Leo: "Wow, that is a beautiful bicycle! My father also has one big red car. We always go to the market or the park on Sunday."
Tias: "That sounds fun! There are ten big trees at the park. Let's go there together next week!"
Leo: "Sure! Let's ride your blue bicycle and my father's red car."
💡 Misi Siswa: Tutor membaca bagian Tias, siswa membaca bagian Leo. Mintalah siswa mencari nama tempat (*places*) dan kendaraan (*transportation*) di teks!

🤔 Refleksi & Kesimpulan Hari Ini

Preposisi Kendaraan (By vs On):

Gunakan "By" untuk kendaraan mekanis:
• By car, By bus, By train.
Pengecualian sepeda/jalan kaki:
• On bicycle / On foot.

Kosakata Penting:

Go to = Pergi ke
Ride = Mengendarai (sepeda)
Drive = Menyetir (mobil)

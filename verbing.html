Spelling Rules

How to Add -ing ✏️

There are 4 simple rules for adding -ing to verbs. Click any card to hear examples!

Rule 1: Most Verbs

Just add -ing

walk → walking
eat → eating
read → reading
play → playing
Rule 2: Ends in -e

Drop the -e, then add -ing

make → making
write → writing
dance → dancing
cooke → cooking
Rule 3: Ends in -ie

Change -ie to -y, add -ing

lie → lying
die → dying
tie → tying
Rule 4: CVC Pattern

Short verb ending in C+V+C: double the last consonant

run → running
swim → swimming
sit → sitting

💡 Quick Tip

CVC means Consonant + Vowel + Consonant — like "run" (r-u-n). For these short verbs, we double the last letter before adding -ing!
But verbs ending in w, x, y don't double: fix → fixing, snow → snowing, play → playing.

Use 1 of 5

Continuous Tense ⏰

Verb + ing shows an action that is happening right now or around now.

Pattern: Subject + am/is/are + Verb + ing

Use am with I, is with he/she/it, are with you/we/they

I am eating breakfast now.
(Saya sedang makan sarapan sekarang.)
She is cooking in the kitchen.
(Dia sedang memasak di dapur.)
They are playing football.
(Mereka sedang bermain sepak bola.)
We are studying English.
(Kami sedang belajar bahasa Inggris.)

🔑 Key Words

now right now at the moment today Look! Listen!

These words often appear with the Continuous Tense to show the action is happening right now.

Use 2 of 5

Gerund 🏷️

Verb + ing can work as a noun (kata benda). It becomes the name of an activity!

As Subject: Verb+ing + is/are + ...
Swimming is fun.
(Berenang itu menyenangkan.)
Reading is my hobby.
(Membaca adalah hobiku.)
Cooking is her favorite activity.
(Memasak adalah aktivitas favoritnya.)
Walking is good for health.
(Berjalan baik untuk kesehatan.)
As Object: Subject + verb + Verb+ing
I like reading books.
(Saya suka membaca buku.)
She loves dancing.
(Dia suka menari.)
My hobby is drawing.
(Hobiku adalah menggambar.)
He enjoys playing football.
(Dia menikmati bermain sepak bola.)

💡 Remember!

When verb+ing works as a gerund, it acts like a noun. It can be a subject (Swimming is fun) or an object (I like swimming). The -ing form names the activity!

Use 3 of 5

After Prepositions 📍

After a preposition (kata depan), we always use Verb + ing — never the base verb!

Pattern: Preposition + Verb + ing
I am good at drawing.
(Saya pintar menggambar.)
She is interested in reading.
(Dia tertarik membaca.)
He is afraid of falling.
(Dia takut jatuh.)
Thank you for helping me.
(Terima kasih telah membantu saya.)
She is good at cooking.
(Dia pintar memasak.)
We are tired of waiting.
(Kami lelah menunggu.)

🔑 Common Prepositions

at in of for about before after without by

Examples: "before sleeping" (sebelum tidur) · "after eating" (setelah makan) · "by walking" (dengan berjalan) · "without drinking" (tanpa minum)

Use 4 of 5

After Certain Verbs 🔗

Some verbs are always followed by Verb + ing — not "to + verb".

Pattern: Certain Verb + Verb + ing
I enjoy reading books.
(Saya menikmati membaca buku.)
She loves dancing.
(Dia sangat menyukai menari.)
He stops talking.
(Dia berhenti berbicara.)
They finish eating lunch.
(Mereka selesai makan siang.)
I practice playing football.
(Saya berlatih bermain sepak bola.)
She keeps drawing pictures.
(Dia terus menggambar gambar.)

🔑 Verbs Always Followed by -ing

enjoy like love stop finish mind practice keep avoid consider

⚠️ Common mistake: "I enjoy to read" ❌ → "I enjoy reading" ✅
With these verbs, always use verb + ing!

Use 5 of 5

As an Adjective 🎨

Verb + ing can describe a noun — like an adjective (kata sifat)!

Pattern: Verb+ing + Noun (describing the noun)
Look at the sleeping cat.
(Lihat kucing yang sedang tidur.)
The running water is cold.
(Air yang mengalir itu dingin.)
I like interesting books.
(Saya suka buku yang menarik.)
This is a boring movie.
(Ini film yang membosankan.)
The crying baby is hungry.
(Bayi yang menangis itu lapar.)
She is a loving mother.
(Dia ibu yang penuh kasih.)

🧠 Summary: The 5 Uses

1️⃣ Continuous Tense: She is cooking. (action now)
2️⃣ Gerund (Noun): Cooking is fun. (subject/object)
3️⃣ After Preposition: good at cooking. (after at/in/of/for)
4️⃣ After Certain Verbs: enjoy cooking. (after enjoy/love/stop)
5️⃣ As Adjective: a cooking smell. (describing noun)

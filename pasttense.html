Grammar Lesson 1

Simple Past Tense 📜

We use Simple Past Tense for actions that happened and finished in the past.

✅ Positive (+)

Subject + V2 (Verb 2)

I played football.
(Saya bermain sepak bola.)
She went to school.
(Dia pergi ke sekolah.)

❌ Negative (-)

Subject + did not (didn't) + V1

I didn't play football.
(Saya tidak bermain sepak bola.)
She didn't go to school.
(Dia tidak pergi ke sekolah.)

❓ Question (?)

Did + Subject + V1?

Did you play football?
(Apakah kamu bermain sepak bola?)
Did she go to school?
(Apakah dia pergi ke sekolah?)

🧠 Time Signals (Keterangan Waktu)

yesterday last night last week last month two days ago in 2010

Important: In negative and question forms, we always use V1 (base verb) after "didn't" or "Did"!
Example: "She didn't go" (NOT "didn't went").

Grammar Lesson 2

Has vs. Have (Possession) 🎒

We use has and have to show possession (kepemilikan). What is the difference?

HAVE

Use with: I, You, We, They (and plural subjects)

I have a new book.
(Saya memiliki buku baru.)
They have two cats.
(Mereka memiliki dua kucing.)
Cio and Sinta have bicycles.
(Cio dan Sinta memiliki sepeda.)

HAS

Use with: He, She, It (and singular subjects)

He has a red car.
(Dia (lk) memiliki mobil merah.)
She has long hair.
(Dia (pr) memiliki rambut panjang.)
The cat has soft fur.
(Kucing itu memiliki bulu lembut.)

⏳ Past Form: HAD

In the Past Tense, both have and has change to had!
I had a toy. (Saya punya mainan.)
She had a toy. (Dia punya mainan.)
⚠️ Difference:
Have/Has = Possession in the Present (Now)
V2 (played, went) = Actions in the Past (Yesterday)

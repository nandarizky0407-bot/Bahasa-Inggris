Introduction

What is a Preposition? 🧭

A preposition is a word that shows the relationship between a noun/pronoun and other words in a sentence. It tells us about place, time, and direction.

📍

Place

Shows where something is.
in, on, at, under, behind, between, next to

The cat is on the table.
⏰

Time

Shows when something happens.
in, on, at, before, after, during

I wake up at six o'clock.
🚶

Direction

Shows where to or where from.
to, into, out of, through, across, along

She walks to school.

💡 Why are Prepositions Important?

Prepositions help us understand where, when, and how things happen. Without prepositions, we wouldn't know if the cat is on the table, under the table, or behind the table! They give us important details about location, time, and movement.

Preposition of Place

Where is it? 📍

These prepositions show position or location.

in = di dalam
The book is in the bag.
(Buku itu di dalam tas.)
on = di atas (menempel)
The cup is on the table.
(Cangkir itu di atas meja.)
at = di (lokasi spesifik)
I am at school.
(Saya di sekolah.)
under = di bawah
The cat is under the chair.
(Kucing itu di bawah kursi.)
behind = di belakang
The ball is behind the door.
(Bola itu di belakang pintu.)
in front of = di depan
The car is in front of the house.
(Mobil itu di depan rumah.)
between = di antara (2 benda)
The pencil is between the books.
(Pensil itu di antara buku.)
next to = di sebelah
I sit next to my friend.
(Saya duduk di sebelah temanku.)
above = di atas (tidak menempel)
The lamp is above the desk.
(Lampu di atas meja.)

🧠 in vs on vs at — What's the difference?

in = di dalam ruang/area
in the room, in the bag, in the park
on = di atas permukaan
on the table, on the wall, on the floor
at = titik lokasi spesifik
at school, at home, at the door
Preposition of Time

When does it happen? ⏰

These prepositions show when something happens.

in

Use with: months, years, seasons, parts of day

in January, in March
(di bulan Januari, Maret)
in 2024, in 2010
(di tahun 2024)
in the morning
(di pagi hari)
in the afternoon, in the evening
(di siang/sore hari)

on

Use with: days of week and specific dates

on Monday, on Friday
(pada hari Senin, Jumat)
on Sunday morning
(pada pagi hari Minggu)
on July 5th
(pada tanggal 5 Juli)
on my birthday
(pada ulang tahunku)

at

Use with: specific clock times

at 7 o'clock
(pada jam 7)
at noon, at midnight
(pada siang/malam hari)
at night
(pada malam hari)
at breakfast time
(pada waktu sarapan)

📋 Other Time Prepositions

before = sebelum
before sleeping
after = sesudah
after breakfast
during = selama
during the break
until = sampai
until 5 o'clock
Preposition of Direction

Where to? Where from? 🚶

These prepositions show movement — where something is going or coming from.

to = ke
I go to school.
(Saya pergi ke sekolah.)
into = ke dalam
She walks into the room.
(Dia masuk ke ruangan.)
out of = keluar dari
He comes out of the house.
(Dia keluar dari rumah.)
through = melalui
We go through the tunnel.
(Kita melalui terowongan.)
across = menyeberangi
We walk across the street.
(Kita menyeberangi jalan.)
along = sepanjang
They walk along the river.
(Mereka berjalan sepanjang sungai.)
up = naik ke
The cat climbs up the tree.
(Kucing naik ke atas pohon.)
down = turun dari
He walks down the stairs.
(Dia turun tangga.)
from = dari
I come from Indonesia.
(Saya berasal dari Indonesia.)

🔑 Other Common Prepositions

with = dengan
I play with my friend.
for = untuk
This gift is for you.
by = dengan (transport)
I go by bus.
about = tentang
a book about animals.

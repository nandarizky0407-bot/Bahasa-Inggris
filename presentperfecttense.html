Grammar Lesson 1

Present Perfect Tense ✨

We use Present Perfect for actions that happened in the past but connect to the present (the result is important now).

✅ Positive (+)

Subject + have/has + V3

I have eaten breakfast.
(Saya sudah makan sarapan.)
She has finished her homework.
(Dia sudah menyelesaikan PR-nya.)

❌ Negative (-)

Subject + haven't/hasn't + V3

I haven't eaten breakfast.
(Saya belum makan sarapan.)
She hasn't finished her homework.
(Dia belum menyelesaikan PR-nya.)

❓ Question (?)

Have/Has + Subject + V3?

Have you eaten breakfast?
(Apakah kamu sudah makan sarapan?)
Has she finished her homework?
(Apakah dia sudah menyelesaikan PR-nya?)

🧠 Time Signals for Present Perfect

already (sudah) yet (belum/sudah) just (baru saja) ever (pernah) never (tidak pernah) since (sejak) for (selama)
Grammar Lesson 2

Present Perfect vs Simple Past ⚖️

What is the difference? Let's compare them side by side!

Simple Past Tense

Action that finished in the past. The time is specific/known.

I ate breakfast at 7 AM.
(Waktunya spesifik: jam 7 pagi)
She went to Bali last year.
(Waktunya spesifik: tahun lalu)

Present Perfect Tense

Action that happened in the past but connects to now. Time is not specific or unfinished.

I have eaten breakfast already.
(Waktu tidak spesifik, yang penting sekarang sudah kenyang)
She has been to Bali twice.
(Pengalaman, waktu tidak disebutkan)

🧠 The Golden Rule

If you mention a specific past time (yesterday, last week, in 2010, two days ago), you MUST use Simple Past.
❌ "I have eaten breakfast yesterday."
✅ "I ate breakfast yesterday."

If you focus on the result/experience without specific time, use Present Perfect.
✅ "I have eaten breakfast."

Grammar Lesson 3

Has/Have: Possession vs Auxiliary 🎒

"Have/Has" can mean possession (kepemilikan) OR act as a helper verb (auxiliary) in Present Perfect.

1. Possession (Kepemilikan)

Meaning: Mempunyai. Followed by a Noun (kata benda).

I have a new book.
(Saya memiliki buku baru.)
She has a red car.
(Dia memiliki mobil merah.)

2. Auxiliary (Present Perfect)

Meaning: Sudah/Belum. Followed by V3 (Past Participle).

I have read the book.
(Saya sudah membaca bukunya.)
She has bought a car.
(Dia sudah membeli mobil.)

⏳ Past Form: HAD

Past Possession:
I had a toy. (Saya punya mainan.)
She had a toy. (Dia punya mainan.)
Past Perfect Action:
I had eaten before you came. (Saya sudah makan sebelum kamu datang.)
⚠️ Summary:
Have/Has + Noun = Kepemilikan (Now)
Have/Has + V3 = Present Perfect (Sudah dilakukan)
Had + Noun = Kepemilikan di masa lalu
